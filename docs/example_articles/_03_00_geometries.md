# Working with Vector Geometries

GeoKit provides comprehensive vector geometry creation and operations capabilities. These capabilties can be separated into two categories:


## [Geometry Creation](./_03_01_create_geometries.md)

Create, visualize and store various geometries.



## [Geometry Operations](./_03_02_geometry_operations.md.md)

Perform various operations on geometries, such as finding their centroid, transforming them into a different spatial reference system, or investigating the spatial relationship between two geometric objects.
